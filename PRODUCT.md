# Product: Global Business Studies — Study Platform

## Product Purpose
A web-based study platform for Global Business Studies students. Helps users study through three modes: flashcards, note summaries, and multiple-choice quizzes.

## Users
University students (18–25) studying global business disciplines. Users study in short focused sessions (15–40 min), often at desks or on laptops. Secondary use on tablets. Motivation: exam preparation, coursework review, and self-testing.

## Register
product (the design serves the study experience — not a marketing page)

## Brand
- **Tone**: Authoritative, academic, clear — not playful or casual
- **Anti-references**: EdTech apps with pastel gamification, neon colour explosion, childlike illustration styles, cartoon characters
- **Inspiration**: The Economist, Linear, financial terminals, academic journals in digital form
- **Voice**: Concise, confident, instructional. No filler phrases. Every label earns its place.

## Subjects
Core: International Trade, Marketing, Finance, Economics (pre-built content)
Extended: Japanese (L1–3), Chinese (L1–3), Research for Business Studies, EQ and PC (L1–3), Business Economics, Pre Seminar, Accounting Advanced, Management

## Strategic Principles
1. Reduce friction to start studying — one click to any study mode
2. Progress visibility matters — students need to feel forward momentum
3. Language toggle (EN/JA) for Japanese course students
4. Extended subjects accept uploaded materials (PDF/images) — no pre-built content
5. Performance and accessibility are non-negotiable — studying on low-end hardware is common

## Tech Stack
React 19, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, React Router v6, Zustand (persisted state)
